# 🔥 Campfire: Cozy Idle

A cozy idle game where you watch over a flickering campfire under the night sky.
Add wood to grow the flame, level up, and unlock new companions and scenery effects.

## Download

Available on Google Play.

[**Get it on Google Play**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.github.axtone6840.campfire)

## Features

- 🔥 **Tend the fire** — add wood and level up
- 🐿 **Animal companions** — Squirrel, Owl, Rabbit, Beaver, Deer guard the flame
- 🌌 **Decorate the scene** — Tree, Lantern, Log Cabin, Tent, Full Moon, Snow, Aurora
- 🌈 **Flame colors** — Drop metals to tint the flame in firework-bright colors
- ⭐ **Night sky** — Shooting stars, real constellations drifting by
- 🌍 **Multi-language** — English / 日本語
